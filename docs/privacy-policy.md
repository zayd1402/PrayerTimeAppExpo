# PrayerTimeApp Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-15

PrayerTimeApp is designed to calculate prayer times, show Qibla direction, schedule local prayer reminders, and let users track prayer activity on their device.

## Data We Process

- Location: used to calculate prayer times, Qibla direction, and location labels. Location is requested only while using the app.
- Prayer activity: prayer completion, qaza status, streaks, and heatmap data.
- Notification preferences: prayer alert settings and Fajr alarm settings.
- App settings: calculation method, madhab, and saved location.

## Storage

PrayerTimeApp stores settings and prayer activity locally on the device using app storage. The current app does not create user accounts, upload prayer activity to a server, sell personal data, or share prayer activity with third parties.

## Location

Location access is used only for prayer-time and Qibla calculations. Users may deny location access; the app falls back to a default location or a previously saved location where available.

## Notifications

Prayer reminders are scheduled locally through the operating system. Notification content is generated on device.

## Third-Party Services

The current release uses Expo and React Native runtime libraries. It does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or third-party mosque search providers.

## Data Deletion

Users can remove local app data by deleting the app from their device. A future release should expose an in-app reset control before public launch if required by store review or product policy.

## Contact

Support contact and hosted privacy policy URL must be filled in before store submission.
