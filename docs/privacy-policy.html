Privacy Policy — PrayerTimeApp

Last updated: June 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PrayerTimeApp ("we", "the app") handles your information. By using PrayerTimeApp, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Overview

PrayerTimeApp is a cross-platform Islamic prayer companion app. It provides prayer times, Qibla direction, Hijri calendar, daily hadith, duas, and worship tracking. The app is designed with a privacy-first, local-only approach.

In short: We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. All your information stays on your device.

2. Information We Access

Location

  • What: Your device's approximate or precise geographic coordinates (latitude, longitude).
  • Why: To calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla direction for your location, and to find nearby mosques from a built-in static list.
  • How: We request location permission through your device's standard permission system. Location data is used only for calculation within the app and is never transmitted to any server.
  • Control: You can deny location permission and manually select a city from the built-in list. The app functions fully without device location access.

Notifications

  • What: Prayer time reminders and adhan alerts.
  • Why: To notify you when it is time for each prayer.
  • How: We request notification permission through your device's standard system. Notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No push tokens are sent to any server.

Local Storage

  • What: Your prayer log, worship tracking data, journal entries, dhikr counts, Quran reading progress, fasting records, zakat calculations, app settings, and theme preferences.
  • Why: To power the app's tracking and personalization features.
  • How: All data is stored locally on your device using MMKV (Mega-MMKV key-value storage). No data is ever transmitted to any server, cloud service, or third party.

3. What We Do NOT Do

  • We do not collect personal data. No account, no email, no name required.
  • We do not transmit data off your device. No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting SDKs.
  • We do not use third-party tracking or advertising SDKs. No Firebase Analytics, no Google Analytics, no Facebook SDK, no AdMob.
  • We do not sell or share your data. There is no server to sell data from.
  • We do not access your microphone, camera, contacts, photos, or motion sensors. The app does not use these capabilities.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

4. Data Storage and Deletion

  • All data is stored locally on your device.
  • Deleting the app permanently removes all your data. There is no cloud backup.
  • You can also clear all data from within the app via Settings > Clear All Data.

PrayerTimeApp is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children. No age restriction is required.

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and notify you within the app if material changes are made.
