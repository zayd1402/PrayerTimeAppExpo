## Full Description

PrayerTimeApp helps Muslims keep prayer at the center of the day with accurate local prayer times, Qibla direction, reminders, Hijri calendar views, and private on-device prayer tracking.

Key features:

- Accurate prayer times based on your location
- Multiple calculation methods and madhab support
- Next-prayer countdown and daily prayer schedule
- Qibla direction with compass support
- Hijri calendar with prayer activity history
- Local prayer tracking, qaza status, streaks, and activity heatmap
- Optional local prayer notifications and Fajr alarm settings
- Private by design: no account required and no prayer activity uploaded

PrayerTimeApp is built for a calm, focused daily worship routine. Your settings and prayer activity stay on your device.
